## About the app
University books are expensive, and many people have books sitting on a shelf that they no longer need. **Kitabi** brings buyers and sellers together in one organized place, instead of endless scrolling through Facebook and WhatsApp groups.

The Android app is coming soon to Google Play.

## Features
- 📸 **Post a listing in a minute**: add photos, price, condition and location.
- 🗂️ **Browse by category**: university, school, novels and literature, history, science and tech.
- 🔍 **Search and filter**: by title or author, plus price, condition and area.
- 💬 **Contact via WhatsApp**: one button opens a direct chat with the seller.
- 👤 **User accounts**: a clear seller identity builds trust.
